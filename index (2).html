Methodology, scoring and data transparency

Definitions used in this challenge dashboard.

Pressure delivery definition

  • Second innings when required run rate is at least 10 runs per over; OR
  • Final five overs when the batting side has already lost at least four wickets.

Ball and bowling treatment

  • Legal balls exclude wides and oversteps/no-balls.
  • Bowling runs include batter runs plus wides/oversteps. Byes and leg-byes are not charged to the bowler.
  • Bowler wickets include caught, bowled, LBW, stumped and hit wicket.
  • Match-specific revised overs and revised targets are used when available; otherwise the default is 20 overs and first-innings total + 1.

Batting score

35% pressure strike-rate percentile + 25% pressure average percentile + 20% inverse dot-ball percentage percentile + 20% pressure-vs-overall strike-rate improvement percentile. Eligibility: at least 60 pressure balls and 8 pressure matches.

Bowling score

35% inverse pressure economy percentile + 30% wickets per 100 pressure balls percentile + 20% pressure dot-ball percentage percentile + 15% overall-economy minus pressure-economy improvement percentile. Eligibility: at least 120 legal pressure balls and 10 pressure matches.

Interpretation and limitations

  • Percentiles are calculated against the full eligible player population in the source dataset, not only these 20 selected players.
  • Scores describe historical pressure performance; they do not predict future IPL performance.
  • Different leagues, roles, batting positions, venues and opposition quality can affect comparisons.
  • Season trend charts require minimum samples (30 pressure balls for batters; 60 legal pressure balls for bowlers).
  • Phase-specific pressure stats and individual best/worst match innings are not present in the current embedded summary and are not invented here.
